Dental Implants in Kakinada

Permanent, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth.

Book an appointmentCall +91 92900 19948

About this treatment

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once it has healed, a crown, bridge or denture is attached to it. Implants look and feel like natural teeth and help keep the surrounding bone healthy.

Our technology partners include Straumann, a well-known implant manufacturer.

Who it is for

  • You are missing one or several teeth
  • Your dentures feel loose or uncomfortable
  • You want a fixed alternative to removable teeth
  • You would rather not file down healthy neighbouring teeth for a bridge

What to expect

  1. Consultation and scansWe examine your mouth, check bone and gum health and plan the treatment digitally.
  2. Implant placementThe implant is placed under local anaesthesia.
  3. HealingThe implant bonds with the bone over a period your dentist will explain.
  4. Crown or bridgeA custom-made tooth is fitted on top so it blends with your smile.

Benefits

  • Looks and feels natural
  • Stable for eating and speaking
  • Does not rely on neighbouring teeth
  • Helps preserve jawbone

Common questions

Am I suitable for an implant?
Most healthy adults are, but you need enough healthy bone and gums. We assess this at your consultation.
How long does the treatment take?
It depends on healing and on whether extra bone support is needed. We will explain the schedule at your consultation.

This page is general information, not a diagnosis. Your dentist will advise you after examining you.
