Good to know

Common questions, honest answers

Do I need a check-up if I have no pain?
Yes. Cavities and gum disease often cause no pain at first. A check-up every six months catches problems early, when treatment is simpler and cheaper.
Consultation: ₹200–₹500
Is teeth cleaning painful? Does it create gaps?
Cleaning is not painful and does not create gaps. It removes hardened deposits brushing cannot reach. You may feel mild sensitivity for a day or two.
Cleaning: ₹1,000–₹2,500
Why do my gums bleed when I brush?
Bleeding gums usually point to plaque build-up or gum infection. It is not normal, but early treatment and professional cleaning can reverse it.
What is the difference between a filling and a crown?
A filling repairs a small area of damage. A crown covers the whole tooth and is used when the tooth is weak, broken or has had a root canal.
Fillings: ₹800–₹3,000 · Crowns: ₹3,500–₹15,000
Zirconia or metal-ceramic crowns: which is right?
Zirconia is metal-free, strong and very natural-looking. Metal-ceramic is durable and more affordable but can show a dark line at the gum over time. We help you choose based on the tooth, your budget and appearance.
Zirconia: ₹10,000–₹15,000 · Metal-ceramic: ₹4,000–₹7,000
Can a broken tooth be saved?
In most cases, yes, using a filling, crown or root canal depending on the damage. The earlier you come in, the better the chance of saving it.
What is the best age for braces?
Ages 10–14 are ideal because the jaw is still growing, but adults can get excellent results at any age. We assess each patient individually.
